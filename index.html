How to play

  1. Each player owns one slice of tire rubber — a chungi rubber. One flick per turn.
  2. Touch your rubber, drag back and let go. Pull further to slide further.
  3. Once you touch your rubber you have 10 seconds to flick. Listen for the ticks — when time runs out it flicks by itself!
  4. Your finger shakes the longer you hold — watch the arrow and let go at the right moment.
  5. Stop on top of a rival's rubber to score. Just touching doesn't count!
  6. Bounce off a rock or brick first and still land on top: Trick shot = 2 points.
  7. 3–4 players: whoever you land on is knocked out of the round. The last one left wins the round.
  8. Slide past the chalk line and you're Out — your rubber goes back and you lose the turn.
  9. Levels: land on all the white rubbers before your flicks run out. Use one flick per rubber for ★★★. Stars unlock new rubbers.
