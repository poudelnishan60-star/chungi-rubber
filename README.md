# चुङ्गी Chungi Rubber

A free game based on the Nepali street game played with slices of tire rubber — the same rubbers that get bundled into a chungi ball.

Put your finger on your rubber, pull back and flick. Stop **on top of** a rival's rubber to win. Bricks, rocks, mud and slippery stone slabs get in the way.

## Features
- 30 levels with stars — land on every white rubber before your flicks run out
- Versus for 2–4 players on one phone, or against the computer (easy, medium, hard); knockout rounds with 3–4 players
- Fair, symmetric boards and obstacles that change every round
- Unlockable rubbers: bicycle tube, truck tire, tractor lug, red inner tube, golden chungi, festival rubber
- No ads, no in-app purchases, no account; the Android app works offline
